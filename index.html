RemindAir

Google Calendar events and reminders, written directly from your Obsidian notes.

What it does

RemindAir is an Obsidian plugin that connects your vault to Google Calendar and Google Tasks. Write an event on any line of a note, and the plugin creates the matching Google Calendar event and links the two together so they stay in sync.

  • Create a Google Calendar event from a line of text, with the line as the event title.
  • Attach a reminder to a line without leaving the note.
  • Edit or delete an event from the note, with a choice to keep or remove the original text.
  • Pick the exact date and time in a calendar view when creating or rescheduling an event.
  • Read Google Tasks and show them alongside your notes.

How it connects

You sign in with your own Google account through Google's standard OAuth consent flow. RemindAir talks directly from your device to Google APIs and does not run a backend service. Access tokens are stored locally in your Obsidian vault, and you can revoke access at any time from your Google Account permissions page.

Privacy

The plugin only reads the Google data it needs to show your events and tasks. It does not sell or share your data, and it does not use it for advertising. The full details are in the privacy policy linked below.
